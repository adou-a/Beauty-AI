pubchem

烟酰胺（Nicotinamide）是维生素 B3 的一种形式，属于 pyridinecarboxamide（吡啶甲酰胺）类化合物；结构上可理解为吡啶环 3 位氢被甲酰胺基取代。
Nicotinamide is a pyridinecarboxamide that is pyridine in which the hydrogen at position 3 is replaced by a carboxamide group
It is a vitamin B3, a pyridinecarboxamide and a pyridine alkaloid. It is functionally related to a nicotinic acid.
烟酰胺（Nicotinamide）是维生素 B3 的一种形式，属于吡啶甲酰胺类化合物，是与烟酸（nicotinic acid）在结构和功能上相关的化合物。

title：Nicotinamide increases biosynthesis of ceramides as well as other stratum corneum lipids to improve the epidermal permeability barrier
Topical application of nicotinamide increased ceramide and free fatty acid levels in the stratum corneum, and decreased transepidermal water loss in dry skin.
Conclusions: Nicotinamide improved the permeability barrier by stimulating de novo synthesis of ceramides, with upregulation of SPT and other intercellular lipids.
外用烟酰胺可改善皮肤屏障功能，并降低干燥皮肤的经皮水分流失。

title：The effect of niacinamide on reducing cutaneous pigmentation and suppression of melanosome transfer
外用烟酰胺可减少面部色素沉着，并提高皮肤亮度。
In the clinical studies, niacinamide significantly decreased hyperpigmentation and increased skin lightness compared with vehicle alone after 4 weeks of use.
Conclusions: The data suggest niacinamide is an effective skin lightening compound that works by inhibiting melanosome transfer from melanocytes to keratinocytes.

### risk
CIR 
url https://cir-reports.cir-safety.org/view-attachment/?id=39cd1a17-8e74-ec11-8943-0022482f06a6
title：Final Report of the Safety Assessment of Niacinamide and Niacin1
Under conditions of this study, 5% Niacinamide in distilled water was not a significant irritant. 在本研究条件下，5%烟酰胺水溶液并不是显著的皮肤刺激物。
All of the formulations had a low irritancy potential所有测试配方均表现出较低的刺激潜力
No relationship between Niacinamide concentration and skin irritation was demonstrated研究未发现烟酰胺浓度与皮肤刺激之间存在明确关系
no significant differences were found in the irritancy potential between the formulas with and without Niacinamide.含烟酰胺和不含烟酰胺的配方之间，在刺激潜力方面未发现显著差异
These data show Niacinamide-containing formulas to be generally well tolerated.这些数据表明，含烟酰胺的配方总体具有良好的皮肤耐受性。
多项人体皮肤试验显示，在所测试的外用条件下，
含烟酰胺的配方总体具有较低的皮肤刺激潜力并具有良好耐受性。
Niacinamide at 1, 2.5, 5, and 10% in distilled water did not elicit any unacceptable stinging response in any panelist.1%、2.5%、5%和10%的烟酰胺水溶液均未在任何受试者中引起不可接受的刺痛
